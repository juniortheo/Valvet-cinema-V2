Our Mission

A Cinema With No Fixed Address

Velvet Cinema is an independent pop-up cinema in Kigali. We curate film screenings paired with music, drinks and bites — a whole evening, not just a movie.

assets/images/about-hero.jpg
Why we do this

Kigali has stories to watch and people who love to gather. We just put the two in the same room.

Velvet started because great films deserve a great room, and because a film watched alone on a laptop is a different thing entirely from a film watched with two hundred strangers on a rooftop.

We have no permanent venue. Instead we borrow rooftops, gardens, courtyards and warehouses across the city, and turn each one into a cinema for a single night. The room is chosen for the film, never the other way round.

Every screening is curated end to end — the film, the opening set, the drinks, the bites, the light. Nothing is off the shelf. We keep the runs small so that each night belongs to the people who are in it.

What a Night Looks Like

From 6:30

Doors & sound

Arrive early, find your spot, get a drink. A live set or vinyl plays while the light drains out of the sky.

At dusk

The film

A short introduction, then the projector. One film, one time, no interruptions, in a room you would not expect.

After

The conversation

The music comes back on and the bar stays open. Most people leave much later than they planned to.
