A curated cinema experience, just for Kigali.

Velvet Cinema was born from a love of cinema and a desire to bring a different kind of film experience to Kigali.

Inspired by iconic arthouse cinemas in cities like Paris and Amsterdam, we set out to fill a gap in Kigali’s growing cultural scene: access to cult classics, independent cinema, and critically acclaimed films that deserve to be experienced on the big screen.

But for us, cinema is about more than just watching a film. It’s about the atmosphere, the people, and the shared experience of discovering a story together.

Through carefully curated screenings, music performances, DJ sets, themed décor, and food and drinks, we transform film screenings into immersive cultural events that bring cinema, art, and entertainment together.

Our vision is to cultivate a community of film lovers, young creatives, and curious minds while contributing to Kigali’s cultural landscape. What begins as a pop-up cinema is building towards something bigger: an established movie theatre that gives great cinema a home in Kigali.
