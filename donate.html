Keep the Projector Running

Ticket sales cover a night. They don't cover the year. Donations pay for licensing, equipment, and the seats we give away free.

Where it goes

Every franc has a seat

One

Film licensing

Screening films legally, so the people who made them are paid. This is our largest cost and the one nobody sees.

Two

Sound & screen

Projector, speakers, screen and cabling — the kit that turns a borrowed rooftop into a cinema for one night.

Three

Free seats

We hold twenty seats at every screening for students and first-time guests. Donations are what keep them free.

Four

New Rwandan work

A small fund that pays local filmmakers a screening fee when we programme their films.
