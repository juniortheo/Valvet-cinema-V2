Good to know

Before you book

Seats

Limited by the room

Each venue holds what it holds — usually 80 to 200. When a night sells out we don't add a second screening.

Arrival

Come early

Doors open ninety minutes before projection. The opening set and the bar are part of the ticket, not an add-on.

Venue

Sent the day before

Exact locations go out by SMS 24 hours ahead. Velvet has no permanent address by design.

Refunds

Transferable

Can't make it? Reply to your confirmation SMS and we'll move your seat to someone else's name, up to 24 hours before.

Groups & private nights
