Our Mission

A cinema should be a room you remember, not just a film you watched.

Velvet Cinema is independent and unfixed. We have no permanent address — instead we borrow rooftops, gardens, courtyards and warehouses across Kigali, and turn each one into a cinema for a single night.

Every screening is curated end to end: the film, the opening set, the drinks, the bites, the light. We keep the runs small so each night belongs to the people in it.
