Coming up

A curated cinema experience just for Kigali

We were inspired by arthouse movie theaters in cities like Paris and Amsterdam. Kigali’s cultural scene is growing, yet access to critically acclaimed films is still limited. Velvet Cinema was created to enrich film culture by showing more memorable iconic films. We screen movies in fun and interactive events with the goal to soon become our own established movie theater.
