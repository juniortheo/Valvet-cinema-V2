About the night

A continent in six parts

Short film is where most directors begin, and where the boldest ideas usually survive intact. This programme gathers six of them — from Dakar, Nairobi, Kigali, Lagos, Cairo and Johannesburg — and runs them back to back without interruption.

The rooftop opens at 6:30 with a live afrobeat set while the sun drops behind the hills. Projection begins at dusk. Afterwards the bar stays open and the music comes back on, so the conversation has somewhere to go.
